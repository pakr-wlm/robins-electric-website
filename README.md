# Robins Electric — Website

Free homepage rebuild for Robins Electrical Services, Inc. (Warner Robins, GA), built as a cold-outreach lead magnet.

**Live file:** [`index.html`](index.html) — a single self-contained file (CSS and all images inlined as base64), so it renders correctly no matter how it's opened: double-clicked, dragged into a browser, or sent as an email attachment.

**Dev source:** `styles.css` and `assets/` hold the editable, non-inlined originals for making future changes. After editing either, re-inline them into `index.html` before sending — see the project's `electrician-website-generator` skill for the build script.

## Known placeholders — not yet real

- Hero and About section photos are temporary stock images (Unsplash, free license), not real photos of the business
- "Recent Work" gallery photos are also temporary stock, each matched to a real service the business offers, but not actual completed jobs
- No formal guarantee/warranty language beyond what's implied by real Google reviews

Swap these for real photos and confirm any additional details with the business before this goes out as the final, permanent site.
